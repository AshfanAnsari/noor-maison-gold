Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Noor Maison by Saba is a jewellery shop at 22-1-809 Noorkhan Bazar, Hyderabad 500024. This page explains, in simple words, what information our website collects and what happens to it.

What we collect

When you place an order: your name, phone number and delivery address. We need these to confirm your order and deliver it to you.

If you sign in with Google (your choice): your name, email address and photo, which Google shares with us when you sign in, plus the phone number and delivery address you save in your Settings on this website.

On your own phone: this website stores your orders and your sign-in on your device itself, so the site works smoothly for you. This stays on your phone.

Why we collect it

Only to take your order, confirm it with you, deliver it, and — if you have an account — to fill your details in for you when you order again. We do not use your information for anything else, and we never sell it to anyone.

Where it is kept

Your information is stored in a secure database hosted in India (Mumbai). Only Saba, the owner of Noor Maison, can see your order and account details.

Your choices

An account is always optional — you can order as a guest any time. You can ask Saba at any time to see your saved details or to delete them completely. Just call 86866 90764 or email noormaisonbysaba@gmail.com, and it will be done.
