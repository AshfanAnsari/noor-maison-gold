How ordering works

Choose a piece, tap Order, and fill in your name, phone number and address. Saba confirms every order personally before anything is sent out.

Payment

Payment is by UPI, after Saba confirms your order. Please keep your Order ID ready when you pay — it helps Saba match your payment to your order.

Prices

All prices are in Indian Rupees (₹). Where a piece shows "Price on enquiry", Saba will tell you its price when she confirms your order.

Delivery

Orders are sent by courier to the address you give us. Shipping is free on orders above ₹499.

Our pieces

Our jewellery is Korean-imported, anti-tarnish fashion jewellery, and our nail sets are press-on nails. The photos show the actual designs; small differences in colour can appear because of phone screens and light.

If something is wrong

If anything about your order is not right, message Saba on Instagram @noormaison_by_saba or call 86866 90764 — she will sort it out with you personally.
